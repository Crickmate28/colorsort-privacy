Color Sort Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Color Sort ("the app") is a color-sorting puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. It is published by Sushil Rewale, contact: sushilrewale17@gmail.com.

Information we do not collect

Color Sort has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to fix bugs, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These collect:

  • Gameplay events: e.g. which level you played, how many moves it took, hints and other helpers used, coins earned and spent, ads watched, purchases started or completed, screens visited;
  • Crash and error reports: the error and a stack trace, recent in-app steps before the crash, device model, OS version, app version;
  • Identifiers: a random Firebase installation ID and, for Analytics, your device's advertising ID; approximate location (country/city) derived from your IP address.

This data is not linked to your name or contact details. It is processed by Google under Firebase's privacy terms and kept for up to 14 months (Analytics) or 90 days (Crashlytics).

Game progress

Your level, coins, daily streak and settings are stored only on your device. If Android Backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this game data in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we cannot see it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address, device model, operating system and app version,
  • ad interactions (views, clicks) and approximate location derived from the IP address.

Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices (ads) (shown only where this choice is required).

Buying "Remove Ads" removes the ads between levels and the banner under the game board. Optional videos you choose to watch for rewards stay available.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We receive only confirmation that a purchase was made, so the app can unlock what you bought.

Children

Color Sort is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

Data deletion

All game data is stored on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Color Sort, deletes it. Analytics and crash data are not linked to your identity and are deleted automatically after the retention periods above. For any privacy request, email the address above.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always at this page, with the date at the top.
